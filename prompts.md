# Torzo Monochrome Illustration Prompts

This document contains **5 highly-calibrated image generation prompts** designed in a modern, minimalist, monochrome line-art editorial style. These are perfect for your media search, debrid cloud manager, and torrent scraping platform, **Torzo**.

### Design Philosophy
- **Colors:** Strictly black, white, and muted grays only.
- **Outlines:** Clean, expressive black ink outlines.
- **Textures:** Halftone dot shading (screentones) for depth and contrast. No flat gray fills or color gradients.
- **Characters:** Quirky, exaggerated proportions.
- **Dynamics:** Loose, floating accent lines (swirls, action markers, stars).
- **Format:** Landscape aspect ratio (16:9) optimized for UI hero sections, empty states, and Open Graph previews.

---

## 1. The Media Finder (Perfect for Search & Discovery)

> **Prompt:**  
> A modern minimalist monochrome editorial illustration in a clean line-art style, featuring a quirky character with highly exaggerated long arms peering through a giant whimsical magnifying glass. The character is looking at miniature floating movie film reels, retro clapperboards, and glowing play buttons. The illustration uses a strict palette of solid black, stark white, and muted grays, relying entirely on halftone dot patterns (screentones) for texture, depth, and shading. Character has solid black hair to ground the composition. Loose, floating accent swirls, action lines, and tiny abstract shapes bounce around to add dynamic movement. Sharp, expressive black ink outlines, set against a solid clean white background. Landscape aspect ratio, 16:9, high resolution, vector graphic style.
